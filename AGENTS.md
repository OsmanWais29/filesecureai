# Agent rules

- Trust-account figures (balance, available-to-distribute, reconciliation state, trust risk flags, SRD) are computed only in `src/lib/trust/trustLedger.ts` from ledger rows; UI never accepts a typed balance. Why: Directive 5R8 requires balances traceable to entries, and Distribution approval gates depend on the same numbers.
- Estate Financials owns trust entry and reconciliation; creditor views use the same estate-scoped persisted postings, never a shared demo store. Why: cash belongs to the estate, and unavailable evidence must block distribution assessment.
- Accounting checks and SRD evidence gates abstain when bank provenance, pooled membership, tariff bases or schedule links are unavailable; posted ledgers are immutable and database-validated. Why: balanced cash alone does not establish regulatory compliance or bank-backed SRD accuracy.
- Trust Account is its own estate module with Today, Ledger, Payments, Month-end and Reports pages, with entry and evidence tools in focused side panels. Why: trustees need a short daily workflow without duplicate financial navigation or permanent exception lists.
- Estate form version, applicability, status, grouping and next action come only from `src/lib/forms/formEngine.ts`; signing refuses until flags are fixed or acknowledged, and "Filed" requires recorded OSB evidence (signing is internal only). Why: trustees must never pick a version, and filings need an auditable reason trail.
- OSB filing state, XML, pre-submission checks and evidence packs come only from `src/lib/filing` and the `osb_filings` tables; the estate Forms panel starts filings but the Filing Centre owns them. Why: filings need one firm-wide queue, database-enforced forward-only status, and sealed, reproducible evidence.
- Estate Financials to-do items, payment performance, payout estimates and income trends come only from `src/lib/financials/financialsEngine.ts`; each figure abstains when its inputs are missing. Why: trustees act on these items, so they must be testable and never guessed.
- Financials AI document reading runs in the `read-income-document` function as the signed-in user and returns draft figures only; saving requires trustee confirmation and lands as a Draft income month. Why: AI output must never feed surplus or filings unreviewed.
- Trust-check inputs (account type, fee authority, tariff/levy bases, proposal schedule, firm approval limit) are entered per estate in `estate_trust_settings`; checks abstain while a needed field is blank. Why: the OSB checks need trustee-recorded facts, never inferred ones.
- Claim priority comes only from `src/lib/creditors/s136.ts`, ranked from the recorded claim type; an unknown type is "needs review". Why: BIA s.136 depends on the claim's nature, not the creditor's name.
